## SAR-Based Crop Phenology Detection
This project used Umbra high-resolution SAR data to investigate crop phenology at a research farm in southwest Michigan. I supported the project through extensive field work and raster processing in Python. We found that while noise caused by variation in the imaging geometry prevented the detection of phenological change through more conventional means, the lacunarity index - a texture metric from the world of fractal geometry - could be used to detect the leaf-out of biofuel poplars. The image below shows the normalized lacunarity index curves of several plots containing poplars in the farm.
This project is being developed into a paper for publication at a later date.
